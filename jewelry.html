Our Heritage

Crafted in the heart of Binondo

Binondo Gold Diamond Jewelry was founded in the world's oldest Chinatown, a district built on generations of gold and diamond trade. Every piece carries that inheritance: exacting craftsmanship, honest weight, and stones chosen with a trader's eye.

Since MMXX, we've served collectors and families who see jewelry not as decoration, but as something passed down.
